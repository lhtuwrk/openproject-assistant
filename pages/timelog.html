How to log time
  • Clicka cell in your row (marked You). Pick a work package from the list (yours first), check the hours, then press Enter to log. Nothing is chosen for you.
  • Shift + Clicka second cell in your row to fill a range of days; Ctrl/Cmd + click adds or removes a single day. Or tick days under Days in the form: All missing picks every day under 8 h. Leave Hours empty to top each day up to 8 h.
  • Shift + Enteradds the ticket to the To log list and opens the next one, so one go logs several tickets (on one day or on several). Enter then logs them all.
  • Editon an entry in the form changes its hours or moves it to another day. Logged the wrong day? Change the date and Save.
  • Esccloses the form. Every log shows a toast with Undo. No cell to click? Use Log time at the top.
