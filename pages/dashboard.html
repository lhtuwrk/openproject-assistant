Settings

Turn each feature on or off. Changes save as you click and reach open backlog tabs the next time they load.

On backlog pages

Runs inside work-package lists and detail views on your backlog. Change host →

  • Splits the Progress bar of each parent work package into its subtasks (done, in progress, open), with a breakdown on hover.

    Work-package lists

  • Adds an Assignee column to the Children table in a work package's Relations tab. Click a name to reassign that child. Works with or without Jira style.

    Relations tab · writes the assignee

  • Adds Show All · Comments · History to a work package's Activity tab, and can hide the automatic progress updates children cause. Works with or without Jira style.

    Activity tab · display only

  • Shows a work package's files as Jira-style cards in the Files tab: a thumbnail for images, a file-type tile otherwise, with size, date and uploader. A List toggle brings back OpenProject's list. Works with or without Jira style.

    Files tab · display only

  • When a Task moves to Resolved, Closed or Done, its progress is set to 100 % in the same save. Reopening it sets progress back to 0 %.

    Status changes · Tasks only

  • Asks before a work package is resolved or closed while some of its subtasks are still open. Cancel keeps the old status.

    Status changes

  • Reuses the work-package list for 15 seconds, so going back from a story doesn't reload it. Saves clear it at once; other people's changes show up after 15 seconds, and OpenProject's refresh button returns the same rows in that time. Turn off to always fetch fresh.

    Work-package list

In the background

Runs while Chrome is open, even with no backlog tab.

  • Saves one snapshot a day of every tracked version, syncs hourly, and rebuilds missed days from the activity feed at midnight.

    Hourly + midnight Open Burndown →

  • On the last working day of each sprint and month, and the day before, you get a "log your time" task until you click Done: a toolbar badge, a popup card, a floating card on websites from 16:00, and a Windows notification at most every 3 hours.

    Sprint + month end

Appearance

  • Theme

    Applies to the popup, every dashboard page and the backlog (with Jira style on).

  • Restyles OpenProject like Jira: a white top bar and light sidebar on every page; work-package lists with status lozenges and type and priority icons; the work-package page with Details in a right-hand sidebar and a Child work items section, where you can change a child's assignee and status; and the Backlogs page as Jira sprint containers. Those two changes are the only things it writes to OpenProject.

    Top bar · work packages · backlogs

  • Slide, fade and hover transitions on OpenProject pages. Turn off if dragging or scrolling feels slow; animations are always paused while you drag.

    Skin · welcome · activity filter

  • Shows a random motivational quote in the bottom bar of the work-package list, a new one each time you open the list. Long quotes are shortened to two lines.

    Work-package list
